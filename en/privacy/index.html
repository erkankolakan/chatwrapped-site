Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 13, 2026

In short: your chat is analyzed entirely on your phone. Your messages, names and chat content are never sent to any server. We only collect anonymous usage statistics and crash reports to understand how the app is used.

1. What this policy covers

This policy explains what data is processed when you use the Chat Wrapped mobile app (the "App") and the chatwrapped.fun website. The developer of the App is the data controller under this policy. You can reach us at destek@chatwrapped.fun.

2. Your chat file

  • The chat export file (.txt or .zip) you load into the App is read and analyzed only on your device.
  • Message content, participant names, phone numbers and the file name are never sent to any server and are not stored on our servers or those of third parties.
  • When you pick a file, the operating system may place a copy in the App's temporary cache on your device. This copy never leaves your device; you can remove it completely by deleting the App.
  • Because you are processing messages written by other people in the chat, the App asks you to confirm that they are aware of this before you load a file.

None of this data contains the content of your chat. We do not collect the advertising identifier (IDFA / Android Advertising ID), and we do not use your data for advertising or sell it. The App contains no ads.

4. Payments

Purchases are made through the Apple App Store or Google Play. Your payment details never reach us; they are processed by Apple or Google under their own privacy policies.

5. Images you share

Share cards are created on your device. If you choose to share a card, the image is sent, at your request, to the app you select (e.g. Instagram). You are responsible for what you share; cards may include the names of people in the chat.

6. Service providers and international transfers

The service providers that process the data above (Google LLC, RevenueCat Inc.) may process it on servers outside your country, mainly in the United States. Their privacy policies:

  • Firebase privacy and security
  • RevenueCat privacy policy

7. Retention

Usage statistics are kept for up to 14 months under Google Analytics' data retention settings, and crash reports for up to 90 days. Subscription records are kept for as long as legal and accounting obligations require.

8. Your rights

Depending on where you live (for example under the GDPR or Turkey's Personal Data Protection Law, KVKK), you have the right to learn whether your data is processed, request information, ask for it to be corrected or deleted, and object to its processing. Send requests to destek@chatwrapped.fun. Because usage statistics are tied to an anonymous ID, you may need to share the App's identifier on your device with your request.

9. Children

The App is not intended for children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect data from this age group.

10. Changes

We may update this policy. Updates are published on this page together with the "Last updated" date.
